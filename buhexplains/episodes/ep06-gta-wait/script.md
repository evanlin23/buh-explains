# ep06-gta-wait

How long have we actually waited for GTA six?
You see, the GTA six we all know and love finally drops November nineteenth.
But GTA five came out in September twenty thirteen.
Since then, we got a whole new pope.
A baby born the day GTA five dropped
is a teenager now. Thirteen years.

Sources:
- gta6_date: Rockstar announced Nov 6, 2025 that GTA VI moved to Nov 19, 2026 (Engadget, Bleacher Report); Take-Two reaffirmed it in 2026 (Northeast Times 2026-07-18).
- gta5_date: GTA V released Sept 17, 2013 (Rockstar).
- pope: Pope Leo XIV elected May 8, 2025.
- thirteen: Sept 17, 2013 to Nov 19, 2026 = 13 years 2 months.

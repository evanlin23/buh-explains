# ep05: how much is mariah carey's 'it's time' worth? (draft 1)

1. How much is Mariah Carey's It's time actually worth?
2. You see, the video we all know and love drops every November first.
3. She's not announcing a tour. Or a new album.
4. She's announcing one song from nineteen ninety four.
5. All I Want for Christmas Is You took twenty five years to finally hit number one.
6. And now it comes back every single December.
7. So The Economist did the math on that one song.
8. About sixty million dollars in royalties by twenty sixteen.
9. That's two and a half million dollars a year.
10. So yes, it's time. Time to get paid.

## Sources
- its_time: Mariah Carey posts an 'It's time' video every Nov 1 (2026 edition: Paris Match 2026-11-01; Know Your Meme traces the defrost joke to 2017-2018).
- number_one: 'All I Want for Christmas Is You' (1994) first topped the Billboard Hot 100 on the chart dated Dec 21, 2019 (Billboard via CBS News 2019-12-16; The Fader).
- royalties: The Economist (2017): about $60 million in royalties 1994-2016, roughly $2.6 million a year (cited by The Independent, Daily Beast).

# ep04: why is 'why would I deceive you' so funny? (draft 1)

1. Why is Why would I deceive you so funny?
2. You see, the meme we all know and love is a woman staring way too deep into a man's eyes.
3. The man is Nathan Fielder. It's from the teaser for his A24 documentary.
4. Is it the stare? The baby noises in the background? Nope.
5. It's who she is.
6. She's Elizabeth Holmes, who founded Theranos, a blood testing company once worth nine billion dollars.
7. Then it all fell apart, and in twenty twenty two she was convicted of defrauding investors.
8. Eleven years in prison.
9. That's why why would I deceive you is so funny.

## Sources
- teaser: A24 teaser for You Can See Everything (Nathan Fielder, Lance Oppenheim), released after the Telluride screening, Sept 2026 (Euronews 2026-09-07; Awards Radar 2026-09-07); Holmes asks Fielder 'Why would I deceive you'; a newborn is heard in the background. KYM: 'Elizabeth Holmes and Nathan Fielder "Why Would I Deceive You?"'.
- verdict: Jan 3, 2022: guilty on four counts (one conspiracy, three wire fraud) of defrauding investors; acquitted on the patient counts (NPR/CT Public, Stanford Daily).
- sentence: Nov 2022: 11 years 3 months in federal prison (NBC, LAist; US Attorney Stephanie Hinds statement).
- valuation: Theranos valued at about $9 billion before the 2015 WSJ exposé (Stanford Daily; GEN).

# ep13-harold

Who is Hide the Pain Harold in real life?
You see, the Harold we all know and love smiles through the pain in every stock photo.
A photographer found his holiday pictures online and asked him to model.
Then in twenty eleven, a forum found the photos and named him Harold.
At first, he wasn't happy about it.
Then he leaned in. Commercials, a TEDx talk, even a music video.
But his name isn't even Harold.
He's András Arató, a retired electrical engineer from Hungary.

Sources:
- meme: Know Your Meme, 'Hide the Pain Harold': stock photos of a Hungarian man on Dreamstime; the meme began on the Facepunch forums in 2011, where users named him Harold.
- person: Wikipedia / Wikiwand, 'András Arató': Hungarian retired electrical engineer from Kőszeg; a photographer saw his holiday photos online and asked him to model for stock photos; he later embraced the meme (commercials, TEDx talk).
- now: Know Your Meme editorial, 'Where Are They Now: András Arató'; Daily Dot and BroBible interviews (he was unhappy at first, then embraced it); YouTube: 'Waking up as a meme-hero | Andras Arato | TEDxKyiv'; Cloud 9+ 'Hide The Pain' (Official Music Video).

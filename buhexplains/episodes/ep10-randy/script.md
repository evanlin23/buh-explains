# ep10-randy

Who is Randy actually squaring up to?
You see, the What's up brah meme we all know and love has Randy circling everyone.
Omni-Man. Minecraft Steve. Anime villains.
But in the original episode from twenty eleven, he's squaring up to a real person.
That old man is Stephen Sondheim, the Broadway legend who wrote the lyrics to West Side Story.
So Randy was bro-ing down with a Broadway genius.

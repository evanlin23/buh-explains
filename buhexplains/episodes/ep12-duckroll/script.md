# ep12-duckroll

What did the internet trick you with before the Rickroll?
You see, the Rickroll we all know and love sends you to Rick Astley.
But before Rick, there was another trap.
Back in two thousand six, four chan had a word filter.
Every time you typed egg, it turned into duck.
So eggroll became duckroll.
People posted links promising something juicy.
But click one, and you got...
a duck on wheels.

Sources:
- duckroll: Know Your Meme, 'Duckroll': a bait-and-switch from 4chan in 2006; moot's word filter changed 'egg' to 'duck', so 'eggroll' became 'duckroll'; the link led to a picture of a duck on wheels. It is the precursor of the Rickroll.
- rickroll: Know Your Meme, 'Rickroll': began on 4chan in May 2007 as a variant of the duckroll, with Rick Astley's 'Never Gonna Give You Up' (1987).
- press: TechCrunch, 'Duckroll' (2008 explainer of the 4chan word filter and the duck on wheels); wordorigins.org, 'Rickroll'.

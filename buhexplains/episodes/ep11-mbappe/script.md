# ep11-mbappe

How much did one meme just cost Kylian Mbappé?
You see, the Mbappé meme we all know and love puts his face on a portrait of Mao.
On a French talk show, he called it the funniest meme he'd ever seen.
Some guy, he said. I think he's a Chinese dictator.
Harmless joke? Not in China, where mocking Mao is a serious taboo.
Real Madrid apologized. So did La Liga.
But the real bill came from sponsors.
At least two Chinese brands dropped him.
On October twenty seventh, Mbappé said sorry too.
One meme, two sponsors gone.

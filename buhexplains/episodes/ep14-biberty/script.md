# ep14-biberty

Who built the mascot the internet wants dead?
You see, the Biberty we all know and love is a fuzzy yellow ball selling insurance.
Liberty Mutual launched him this July.
Even his name is a mistake.
In a twenty nineteen ad, an actor tripped over the company name and said Liberty Biberty.
But the hate didn't stay online.
On October twenty fifth, dozens protested outside Liberty Mutual's Boston headquarters.
Their signs said Liberty from Biberty.
So who actually made this thing?
Jim Henson's Creature Shop. Yes, the Muppets guy.
His studio built the most hated mascot on the internet.

# ep07-tralala

Where did melting SpongeBob actually come from?
You see, the Tra La La SpongeBob we all know and love blew up on X on October second.
The song is two kids' shows mashed together: Mickey Mouse Clubhouse and the Banana Splits, from nineteen sixty eight.
The SpongeBob was traced from a Cartoon Rap Battle from twenty nineteen.
But some of the animation came from Get Out of My Car, a cartoon by PsychicPebbles.
And that cartoon was based on a real video from twenty sixteen.
An Uber driver, yelling at his passenger to get out of his car.
So Tra La La SpongeBob is partly an Uber ride.

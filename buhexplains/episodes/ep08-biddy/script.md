# ep08-biddy

What is Biddy Blas actually yelling?
You see, the Biddy Blas we all know and love is the loudmouth racer from Star Wars Galactic Racer.
And his catchphrase is two words. Scrib doon.
He's voiced by a real British pro wrestler, Richard Summers-Calvert.
And scrib doon isn't gibberish. It's Huttese, the language Jabba speaks.
It means write it down.
So Biddy Blas is just telling you to write it down.

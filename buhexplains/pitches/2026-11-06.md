## Recommended

### 1. Remember November 2026 (time-critical: the month is now)
- **Hook / title:** "Do you know the November 2026 polar bear is from 2023?"
- **Reveal:** the "Remember November 2026" bear began as an AI image on TikTok in March 2023, captioned in Russian as a November-2026 meme "you won't understand yet". It came back on 2026-08-28 when @bigbakdiskk28 posted an AI video of the bear singing. And nothing actually happens in November 2026: the joke is the countdown itself.
- **Why now:** it's November 6, 2026, so interest peaks this month and dies in December. The top videos have 4.0M, 2.6M and 582K views.
- **Sources:** [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/remember-november-2026-is-coming) (marked "being researched"), [KYM explainer](https://trending.knowyourmeme.com/editorials/guides/what-is-the-remember-november-2026-meme-the-viral-ai-polar-bear-memes-from-the-future-explained). The fact-checker must pin the March 2023 post: the only timeline source found so far is a fan site.
- **Risks:** the revival video uses Oliver Tree's "Life Goes On". Never use that audio. The bear images are AI-made by others: show them briefly, with our narration on top.

### 2. Verity (strongest demand)
- **Hook:** "Do you know Verity is from Minecraft?" (or "…isn't from The Amazing Digital Circus?")
- **Reveal:** the yellow smiley comes from a Minecraft horror series by YouTuber ThatMob, where Verity is an in-game AI assistant (like Clippy) that turns into a tall monster. It blew up from Horror Skunx's July 2026 song "It's Me, It's Verity". Kids now call any yellow smiley "Verity" and add "-ity" to words ("lovity", "madity"). It has nothing to do with the Anne Hathaway film *Verity*.
- **Demand:** the song has 57M views, and other Verity songs 31M each. Reuters coverage calls it possibly "the new 6-7".
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Verity (meme)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verity_(meme)), [Forbes, 2026-09-27](https://www.forbes.com/sites/danidiplacido/2026/09/27/tiktoks-its-me-its-verity-meme-explained/), [Yahoo: not the movie](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/article/a-verity-meme-is-outshining-the-verity-movie-they-have-nothing-to-do-with-each-other-205701699.html). The sources disagree on the date of the first ThatMob episode: pin it to the upload.
- **Bonus:** a Minecraft origin ties in with @HelloBuh's audience. Cross-promotion is fine; cross-posting isn't.

### 3. The Last Dance
- **Hook:** "Do you know the Last Dance song is 12 years old?"
- **Reveal:** the TikTok duet-dance trend (from Aug 2026) uses "fpd ff7 botsu 8" by Vocaloid producer MARETU, uploaded to SoundCloud on 2014-09-19. "The Last Dance" is a fan nickname. *Botsu* (没) is Japanese for scrapped or rejected; the fact-checker must confirm that this reading of the title is sourced.
- **Demand:** the fan PV has 1.05M views and the SoundCloud re-upload 530K; KYM published its guide on 2026-11-03.
- **Sources:** [KYM: The Last Dance Trend](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/the-last-dance-trend), [KYM explainer](https://trending.knowyourmeme.com/editorials/guides/what-is-the-last-dance-trend-on-tiktok-the-maretu-song-fpd-ff7-botsu-8-and-animation-memes-explained).

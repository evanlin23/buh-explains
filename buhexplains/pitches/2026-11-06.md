# BuhExplains scout, 2026-11-06

What worked before: Tung ("…isn't italian?", 108K views) beat the other two uploads 11–49× because its title made a **surprising claim viewers could check**. That pulled agreement and corrections into the comments. Every pitch below follows that pattern: a hook question, then a sourced reveal.

Demand = the top YouTube results for the topic (yt-dlp search, 2026-11-06).

## Recommended

### 1. Remember November 2026 (time-critical: the month is now)
- **Hook / title:** "Do you know the November 2026 polar bear is from 2023?"
- **Reveal:** the "Remember November 2026" bear began as an AI image on TikTok in March 2023, captioned in Russian as a November-2026 meme "you won't understand yet". It came back on 2026-08-28 when @bigbakdiskk28 posted an AI video of the bear singing. And nothing actually happens in November 2026: the joke is the countdown itself.
- **Why now:** it's November 6, 2026, so interest peaks this month and dies in December. The top videos have 4.0M, 2.6M and 582K views.
- **Sources:** [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/remember-november-2026-is-coming) (marked "being researched"), [KYM explainer](https://trending.knowyourmeme.com/editorials/guides/what-is-the-remember-november-2026-meme-the-viral-ai-polar-bear-memes-from-the-future-explained). The fact-checker must pin the March 2023 post: the only timeline source found so far is a fan site.
- **Risks:** the revival video uses Oliver Tree's "Life Goes On". Never use that audio. The bear images are AI-made by others: show them briefly, with our narration on top.

### 2. 6-7
- **Hook:** "Do you know 6-7 is about a basketball player?"
- **Reveal:** it comes from Skrilla's drill track "Doot Doot (6 7)" (official release 2025-02-07). It spread through edits of LaMelo Ball, who is 6'7". Skrilla says he never gave it a meaning.
- **Demand:** "67 explained" videos get 1.2–1.4M views; it's still alive with kids (TikTok pages updated Nov 2026).
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: 6-7 meme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-7_meme), [Bleacher Report](https://bleacherreport.com/articles/25240758-how-nba-star-lamelo-ball-accidentally-sparked-tiktok-6-7-meme), [Complex](https://www.complex.com/music/a/khal/skrilla-6-7-meme-explained).
- **Risks:** a crowded topic from 2025, so the claim must be sharp. Heavy kid audience: set Made for kids = No and keep it teen-pitched. Don't use the song.
